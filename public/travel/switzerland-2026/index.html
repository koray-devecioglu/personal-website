Swiss Half Fare Card

CHF 150 + tickets

Valid for one month and gives up to 50% reduction on trains, buses, boats and many mountain railways.

  • Estimated trip total: roughly CHF 249–271
  • Usually not worthwhile for only these three regional days
  • Useful only if you add substantial extra Swiss travel

Swiss Travel Pass · 3 days

≈ CHF 254

Unlimited national trains, buses and boats, but most mountain excursions receive a discount rather than full inclusion.

  • Designed for nationwide public-transport travel
  • Your car replaces the expensive intercity journeys
  • Mountain supplements make it a weak fit for this plan
