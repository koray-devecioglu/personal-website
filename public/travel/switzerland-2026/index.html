Weather strategy

Keep the ridge day movable.

Use the clearest, calmest morning for Augstmatthorn. Mürren and Wengen work better on mixed-weather days because the route has easier escape options.

Transport decision

Individual tickets are the sensible default.

Because the car covers the intercity journey and the plan uses only three regional transport days, a nationwide Swiss Travel Pass is unlikely to repay its price.

Recommended

Do not buy the Swiss Travel Pass yet.

Budget roughly CHF 185–225 per adult for the listed mountain transport. Buy point-to-point tickets after the weather order is clear.
