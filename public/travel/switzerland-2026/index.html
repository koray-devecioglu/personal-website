≈ CHF 254

Emergency numbers

  • 112 — general emergency, works everywhere in Switzerland and Germany
  • 1414 — Rega air rescue (Swiss mountains); +41 333 333 333 from a foreign SIM
  • Switzerland: 117 police · 118 fire · 144 ambulance
  • Install the Rega app before the hike — it sends your coordinates with the call
